# Ruben Hassid

## Overview

AI consultant and writer; author of the *How to AI* Substack newsletter (~520K weekly readers as of May 2026). Advises Fortune 500 companies on AI workflow design. Splits time between Paris, Seoul, Berlin, and Tel Aviv.

## Key Facts

- **Newsletter:** *How to AI* on Substack
- **Audience:** ~520,000 weekly readers (May 2026)
- **Focus:** Practical AI workflows for individuals and enterprise teams; voice capture; portable AI-ready personal knowledge files
- **Signature thesis:** A person's voice, taste, and decision-making style can be compressed into a 2,000–5,000 token markdown file that works across any frontier LLM

## Positioning

Sits in the practitioner-educator lane — closer to operators like [[hattie-the-pmm]] than to academic AI commentators. Distinguishes himself by methodology specificity: he publishes step-by-step processes (interview structures, file formats, recommended tools) rather than abstract takes.
