# Mina (@daccord7)

## Overview

Product marketing writer publishing on Medium. Focuses on PMM organizational dynamics, role definition, and the structural forces that dilute PMM impact. Known for naming the "Death by Adjacency" failure mode — scope creep driven by proximity to revenue-critical workflows rather than intentional role expansion.

## Key Facts

- **Platform**: Medium (@daccord7)
- **Focus area**: PMM role scope, organizational design, strategic vs. operational ownership
- **Coined term**: "Death by Adjacency" — overload resulting from proximity to critical organizational workflows

## Positioning

Analytical take on structural PMM problems rather than tactical career advice. Sources industry data (Product Marketing Alliance surveys) to validate qualitative observations, which distinguishes this work from opinion-only PMM content.
