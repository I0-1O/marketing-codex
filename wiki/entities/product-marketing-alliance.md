# Product Marketing Alliance

## Overview

Industry association and research organization focused on product marketing. Produces annual state-of-the-market surveys, benchmarking data, and community resources for PMM practitioners. Primary source for quantitative data on PMM role scope, compensation, team structure, and function ownership.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: Industry association / research body
- **Focus**: Product marketing practitioners globally
- **Notable output**: Annual surveys on PMM role scope, function ownership, team structure
- **2024 data point**: PMMs commonly work across "8–12 distinct functional areas, with many reporting unclear ownership boundaries"

## Positioning

The go-to citation for quantitative PMM role research. Useful when making a case about industry-wide trends rather than anecdotal experience. Data skews toward practitioners who are already engaged with the PMM community, so findings likely understate dysfunction in less-organized PMM functions.

## Competitive Context

Comparable to Forrester or Gartner for the PMM-specific function — a body that publishes benchmark data the field cites to validate or challenge internal assumptions.
